Tips for Best Results

Google Document Setup

  • Ensure file is publicly viewable or shared
  • Include clear research objectives in your document
  • Add specific topics or keywords you want to research
  • Provide context and background information

Optimization Tips

  • Use specific, academic terminology in your document
  • Include related keywords and phrases
  • Specify time periods if relevant to your research
  • Add industry or domain context for better results

How to Find Your Google File ID

1 Open your Google Doc/Sheet in your browser
2 Look at the URL in your address bar
3 Copy the long string after "/d/" and before "/edit"

Example: docs.google.com/document/d/1BxiMVs0XRA5nFMdKvBdBZjgmUUqptlbs74OgvE2upms/edit
